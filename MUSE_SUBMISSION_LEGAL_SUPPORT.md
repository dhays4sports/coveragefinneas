# CF-MUSE-LEGAL-SUPPORT-1.0

Version: 3.20.253

Purpose: make the existing CoverageFit public deployment ready to supply Muse/agent-platform legal and support URLs without inventing a dedicated support mailbox.

## Canonical URLs

- Support: https://coveragefit.com/support/
- Privacy: https://coveragefit.com/privacy/
- Terms: https://coveragefit.com/terms/

## Scope

- Adds the `/support/` page with a support form and the existing agency call/text route.
- Expands the privacy policy for agent/connector-originated requests and third-party agent-platform boundaries.
- Adds `/support/` to the sitemap.
- Leaves the Terms of Use unchanged.
- Does not enable agent execution, change consent rules, add a dedicated support email, or submit a connector to Muse.
## Current technical review surfaces (v3.20.254)

- API: https://coveragefit.com/api/agent
- OpenAPI: https://coveragefit.com/openapi.json
- Documentation: https://coveragefit.com/docs/agent-api/
