The Workspace opens in Today unless a direct consultation link is present. Use the Today, Inbox, Consultation, and Pipeline tabs to move through the Workspace; Left and Right Arrow, Home, and End move between tabs. Inbox summary buttons filter the existing review queue, while delivery, stage, and follow-up filters are available under More filters. Opening a homeowner review moves focus to the Consultation tab. The active homeowner snapshot provides call, text, email, document, and Snapshot actions using the selected consultation. On narrower screens its review facts can be expanded or collapsed, consultation stages form a horizontal swipeable list, and a bottom action dock keeps Call, Text, the current stage, and More actions reachable. Left and Right Arrow, Home, and End also move through Focus Mode stages. Workspace tools, connection setup, conversation readiness, and homeowner document details use expandable controls. Checklist changes are saved automatically. Press Alt+R to refresh Workspace data or Alt+C to show or hide detailed steps.
