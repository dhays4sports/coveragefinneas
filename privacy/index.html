Privacy

Effective: September 19, 2026

Information you provide

CoverageFit may collect assessment answers, contact information, scheduling preferences, and other information you voluntarily submit. Information may be used to generate an educational report or experience, maintain continuity, support a requested licensed review or callback, operate the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and improve CoverageFit.

Agent and connector-originated requests

If you choose to use CoverageFit through a third-party AI assistant, agent, connector, or similar platform, CoverageFit may receive the information that you or that platform intentionally send to fulfill your request. This can include structured request details, a contact reference, requested timing, source or referral attribution, and technical metadata needed to authenticate and process the request.

Third-party agent platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms. CoverageFit does not treat a platform's ranking, listing status, or consent-like metadata as independent authorization for insurance binding, payment, or unrelated contact. CoverageFit applies its own consent and authorization checks before performing supported actions.

Contact preferences and consent

If you ask for a call, text, or email, CoverageFit and the identified agency may use the contact method you selected for that requested purpose. Where the experience provides a way to cancel or change a contact request, that choice is preserved by the applicable workflow.

Producer deployments

The producer identified on the experience may receive submitted information. Each deployment should identify the applicable producer and agency before public use. CoverageFit is currently operated through Virginia Tam Insurance Agency, Inc. for the California consumer experience shown on this site.

Service providers and integrations

CoverageFit may use hosting, forms, messaging, scheduling, analytics, CRM, and other service providers to operate requested workflows. Information is shared with those providers only as reasonably necessary to provide or support the applicable service, subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Sensitive information

Do not send Social Security numbers, payment-card details, passwords, private keys, carrier-portal credentials, or sensitive policy documents through a general CoverageFit form, agent connector, or support request unless CoverageFit explicitly provides a secure workflow for that information.

Educational purpose

CoverageFit does not verify insurance coverage and is not itself a carrier, binder, or coverage determination. Insurance-specific recommendations, eligibility, pricing, binding, and issued coverage remain subject to the applicable carrier, policy documents, underwriting requirements, and licensed review.

Security and retention

CoverageFit uses administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the service, including bounded access and authenticated service requests for agent-originated capabilities. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain operational records, prevent abuse, satisfy legal or business requirements, and support continuity of the consumer or producer workflow.

Contact

For privacy questions, technical help, or issues with an agent-originated CoverageFit request, visit CoverageFit Support.

CoverageFit is an educational self-assessment, not insurance advice, a quote, or a coverage determination. Actual coverage is governed by the issued policy, endorsements, exclusions, limits, deductibles, eligibility rules, and underwriting requirements.
