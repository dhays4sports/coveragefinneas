    Safeco nonrenewal in California: the important distinctions

    Is Safeco leaving California entirely?

    No. The documented withdrawal relevant to this page is narrower: Safeco companies filed to cease writing legacy HO-6 condominium business in California, with new business ending January 1, 2025 and nonrenewals beginning January 1, 2026. Liberty Mutual also retired the Safeco brand in April 2026, but Liberty Mutual stated the brand transition itself did not change existing customer policies.

    What if my notice is about a house or rental property?

    Do not assume it is part of the legacy condo-program withdrawal. A property can be nonrenewed for other underwriting, property, geographic, claims, or program reasons. Use the reason printed on your actual notice when possible.

    How soon should I start?

    The right urgency depends on the effective date shown on the notice. CoverageFit's timing result is operational only: it helps you decide when to organize a review, not whether a carrier will write the property.

    What information should I gather?

    • The actual nonrenewal or cancellation notice
    • Your current declarations page
    • Basic property facts and any material updates
    • Any inspection or roof information specifically mentioned in the notice
    • The exact expiration/effective date

    Can CoverageFit tell me if Farmers will accept the property?

    No. The displacement intake deliberately does not make a Farmers eligibility, underwriting, pricing, coverage, or binding decision. It organizes context so a licensed review can start with fewer repeated questions.
