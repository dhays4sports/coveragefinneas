    Nonrenewal and cancellation are not the same thing.

    A nonrenewal generally means a carrier does not intend to continue the policy for another term. A cancellation generally ends coverage before the normal expiration date. The exact notice and effective date matter, so keep the letter available and use the date shown on it when you can.
